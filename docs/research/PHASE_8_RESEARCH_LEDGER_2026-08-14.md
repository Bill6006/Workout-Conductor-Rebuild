# Phase 8 research ledger — workout intelligence

Status: YELLOW implementation evidence. Last reviewed 2026-08-14 (America/New_York).

This ledger records how published evidence constrains the local recommendation engine. It does not turn population averages into promises for an individual athlete. Workout Conductor remains a planning aid, not medical care, diagnosis, rehabilitation, or a guarantee of hypertrophy.

## Engine interpretation

- Rolling windows: seven days supplies current effective-volume context; 14 and 28 days supply exposure, continuity, and explanation context.
- A second workout on the same day receives a later `workoutPosition`, includes completed earlier work, and may change muscle priorities, exercise choices, set count, and technique use.
- Time selections (15, 30, 45, 60, or profile default) are hard planning ceilings with a small estimator tolerance, not invitations to fill time with arbitrary sets.
- Auto chooses the best supported structure that fits constraints. Straight, Superset, and Drop-set modes alter the engine constraints but cannot bypass equipment, pain, recovery, readiness, or time guardrails.
- Completed working sets count as base evidence. Warm-ups, skipped/omitted exercises, malformed legacy repetitions, and drop-set intensity records do not inflate base progression, PR, or weekly-volume calculations.

## Review policy

Re-review this ledger when a recommendation rule changes, when a source is retracted or materially superseded, and at least once per major release. Record the review date, source link/identifier, product interpretation, and uncertainty. Do not silently convert a research association into an individualized guarantee.
